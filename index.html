What is Color by Number?

Pixel art is a digital art form where images are built pixel by pixel, offering a charming, retro aesthetic. Color-by-Number (or Paint-by-Number) takes this concept into the physical world. It maps an image to a numbered grid, where each number corresponds to a specific color palette. By following the guide, anyone can recreate complex, beautiful artwork flawlessly. It's a highly relaxing, stress-relieving activity perfect for artists and hobbyists of all ages!

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is PixelMe really free to use?

Yes! Our Basic plan is 100% free and allows up to 10 automated image conversions and 1 blank canvas per day.

2. What is a Paint-by-Number (PBN) grid?

It's a generated template where your image is converted into outlines with numbers. Each number corresponds to a specific color in the palette, making it easy to paint or color physically.

3. Can I use PixelMe on my phone or tablet?

Absolutely! Our interface is fully responsive, so you can generate, edit, and draw pixel art right from your mobile browser.

4. How do I print my generated artwork?

Click "Download Workspaces", select your paper size (like Letter 8.5"x11"), and export as a PDF. It will be perfectly scaled for your home printer.

5. Can I change the colors if I don't like them?

Yes! With the Economy or Premium plans, you can easily edit the generated palette, swap hex codes, or add entirely new custom colors.

6. What file formats are supported for upload?

We support all standard web image formats including PNG, JPG, JPEG, and WEBP.

7. Will I lose my work if I switch tabs?

No! Switching between Auto Pixel Art and Blank Canvas keeps your work safe. However, we recommend downloading your workspaces before closing the browser completely.

8. How does the Unlimited Blank Canvas work?

Premium users bypass the daily canvas limits entirely. You can create as many blank grids as you want and use all our drawing tools to create art from scratch.
